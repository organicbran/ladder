Ladder Game

How to play:
WASD to move
SPACE to jump
SHIFT to crouch/slide
WALL RUN by holding into walls
WALL JUMP by jumping on a wall

CLICK to pickup ladder
LEFT CLICK to melee with ladder
RIGHT CLICK to place ladder (hold to throw)

Pickup the ladder in midair by jumping, then grabbing it
Gain backwards momentum off a bounce pad by throwing the ladder,
then catching it as it bounces back while in midair

Get to the goal! You'll also lose if you drop your ladder
